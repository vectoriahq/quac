# QUAC logo

A duck swimming, seen from above and heading top-left. Its head is the diagonal element of the QGIS logo, kept at that element's width with its chevron and two-face shading: an orange bill with a semicircular tip and two small nostrils near its base, a yellow chevron, and a green head, semicircular at the back, with two dark, lens-shaped eyes, each with a white highlight. The brown body's front edges carry the yellow chevron's back edge straight on past the head; the body then rounds into a teardrop that tapers to a pointed tail. Two darker folded wings lie on the back, with a narrow V of body showing between them, each with a large step in its outer edge two-thirds of the way back, forming a second feather point, and their tips meeting just short of the tail. Every part is shaded in two halves, the right-hand half lighter.
